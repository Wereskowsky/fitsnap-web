Privacy Policy

FitSnap in Telegram (bot @FitSnapApp_bot and its Mini App) · version 1 · as of 26 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

[Full name], [Postal address], e-mail: tapakutaba@gmail.com ("we"). See also the Impressum.

2. The short version

  • We store your profile, food diary and allergy list on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, to calculate your daily targets and show your diary.
  • Food photos are analysed by our AI provider and are not stored by us.
  • We show no ads and use no analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your data at any time: Mini App → Profile → Delete my data.

3. Opening the bot and the Mini App

Telegram is a separate service with its own privacy policy; it is responsible for your Telegram account and chats. When you open the Mini App, Telegram passes us data signed with our bot's key: your Telegram user ID, first and last name, username, language and whether you have Telegram Premium. We use it to recognise you without a password. We store only your Telegram ID and language; your first name is suggested in the profile form and stored only if you save the form.

The files of the Mini App are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the page. When you send a command to the bot, Telegram delivers the message to our server, which answers it; we do not store bot messages.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Profile, diary and allergies

We process your name, sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, allergies and intolerances, the language you chose, the version of the terms you accepted, your plan and its end date, and your food diary (foods, portions, nutrients, meal, date, barcode and allergen hints of each entry). We use them to calculate your daily targets, show your diary and history, warn you about your allergens and provide your plan.

Body data, allergies and the diary are data concerning health. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give in the Mini App before anything is saved. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data (section 10); this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. Everything else in this section is processed to perform the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Storage: Supabase, Inc. (USA) as our processor, database region Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Access from outside the EU for support and maintenance is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement.

5. Photo recognition (paid plans)

When you take a photo of a meal, the Mini App reduces it to about 1000 pixels on your phone and sends it, together with the app language and the date, to our server, and from there to Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) Private Limited, which runs the AI model (Qwen) that recognises the food. Alibaba Cloud processes the photo as our processor in Singapore. The transfer outside the EU is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not store the photo. We keep the number of scans per day and technical usage records (model, token counts, cost) to enforce the daily limits and to calculate prices.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Do not photograph people, documents or anything else that is not food.

6. Barcode lookup

When you look up a barcode, the Mini App asks Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts association, France) for the product directly from your phone. Only the barcode number is sent, and Open Food Facts sees your IP address as with any website request. If you scan with the camera, the image is analysed on your phone and never leaves it.

7. Payments in Telegram Stars

Plans are paid in Telegram Stars. Telegram processes the payment; buying Stars themselves is a matter between you and Telegram, Apple or Google. From Telegram we receive the payment ID, plan, amount in Stars, date and your Telegram ID. We use them to activate your plan, handle refunds and keep our accounts (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

8. Support

If you write to support through /paysupport in the bot, the bot forwards your message with your Telegram ID, name and username to our own Telegram account, and our answer goes back through the bot. If you write by e-mail, we process your address and message. We use them only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete support conversations at the latest 12 months after the matter is closed.

10. Deleting your data

In the Mini App: Profile → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your profile, diary, allergies, plan, scan counters and usage records from our server. Payment records stay for refunds and accounting as described in section 9; a paid plan ends with the deletion. Your chat with the bot is stored by Telegram; you can delete it in Telegram. You can also ask us to delete your data by e-mail.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to tapakutaba@gmail.com or use /paysupport in the bot. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

12. Users outside the EU

Data of all users, including users in Russia and Belarus, is stored in Germany as described above.

13. Age

FitSnap is intended for people aged 16 and over.

14. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the Mini App will show the new version before you continue.
