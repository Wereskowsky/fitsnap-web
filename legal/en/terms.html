1. What FitSnap is

FitSnap is a food diary for general wellbeing: it helps you log meals, estimate calories and nutrients and see allergen hints. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Targets and values are guidance figures for healthy adults. The food diary, barcode lookup and history are free. Photo recognition of meals is available in paid plans.

3. Where the data comes from

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free database created by volunteers and licensed under the Open Database License. Dishes, portions and calories recognised from photos are estimates made by an AI model. Calorie targets are calculated with the Mifflin–St Jeor formula. All of these can differ significantly from reality.
